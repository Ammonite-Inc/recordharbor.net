The profile underneath

GEOCEL™

Where a record was, when it was true, and who said so.

GEOCEL™ is Ammonite Intelligence’s geospatial and tritemporal profile of OCEL 2.1, the open standard for object-centric event logs. Geometry belongs to the object and changes over time. Every fact carries three clocks. Nothing is overwritten.

What Record Harbor keeps to

  • Reviewed, not inferred

    Every translation is a mapping a person approved. The product executes it; it never guesses one.

  • Sources named

    Each dataset names its original publisher. Unresolved rights stay visible in the reference.

  • Evidence in view

    Every drawn link carries the documents behind it. A connection without evidence is not drawn.
